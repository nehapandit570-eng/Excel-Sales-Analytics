# 📊 Excel Analytics – Sales & Financial Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the sales and financial performance of AtliQ Hardwares using Power BI reports.

The objective is to understand business performance, monitor sales growth, evaluate profitability, compare market performance against targets, and identify important business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze yearly sales performance.
- Track Net Sales, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), and Gross Margin.
- Compare sales performance across different countries and markets.
- Identify customer-wise sales performance.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Compare actual market performance against sales targets.
- Identify areas that need business improvement.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- DAX – Business calculations and measures
- Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Net Sales increased from ₹87.5M in 2019 to ₹598.9M in 2021.
- Net Sales grew by 204.5% in 2021 compared to 2020.
- Gross Margin percentage decreased from 37.3% in 2020 to 36.4% in 2021.
- India recorded the highest Net Sales among the listed markets in 2021, at 161.26M.
- The overall market performance was 9.17% below the 2021 sales target.
- Poland recorded the highest percentage shortfall against its target among the listed countries.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus on improving sales performance in markets below their targets.
- Investigate the reasons behind the decline in Gross Margin percentage.
- Develop strategies to improve profitability.
- Monitor monthly sales trends to identify opportunities for growth.
- Evaluate customer performance to identify high-value customers.
